Welcome to DrugAware Uganda

As DrugAware Uganda,we aim to provide Ugandans and especially young adults credible information about drug addiction in Uganda.

Drug Addiction Statistics in Uganda

Approximately 1.5 million people in Uganda are estimated to be addicted to drugs.

The most commonly abused drugs include marijuana, alcohol, and khat.

Drug addiction is a leading cause of health problems and social issues in Uganda.

There is a significant increase in drug addiction among young adults in Uganda.

Our Mission

To raise awareness about the dangers of drug addiction and provide support to those affected.
