Warning Signs of Drug Addiction

Recognizing the warning signs of drug addiction can help you or someone you know get the help needed. Here are some common warning signs to look out for:

  • Changes in Behavior:
    Sudden changes in behavior, such as increased secrecy, withdrawal from friends and family, or a decline in performance at work or school.
  • Physical Symptoms: Unexplained weight loss, bloodshot eyes, frequent nosebleeds, or changes in sleep patterns.
  • Financial Issues: Unexplained need for money, borrowing money frequently, or stealing to support drug use.
  • Neglecting Responsibilities: Neglecting work, school, or family responsibilities due to drug use.
  • Risky Behaviors: Engaging in risky behaviors such as driving under the influence or sharing needles.
