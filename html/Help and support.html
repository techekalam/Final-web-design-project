Help and Support

If you or someone you know is struggling with drug addiction, there are resources available to help. Here are some organizations and hotlines that can provide support and assistance:

  • National Drug Helpline: Call 0800 123 456 for confidential support and advice.
  • Uganda Addiction Support Center: Visit their website at www.uasc.org for information on treatment options and support groups.
  • Local Rehabilitation Centers: Contact your local healthcare provider for information on rehabilitation centers in your area.
  • Support Groups: Join support groups such as Narcotics Anonymous (NA) or Alcoholics Anonymous (AA) for peer support and guidance.
